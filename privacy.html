JOYSTICKD

Privacy

Effective September 2026 · joystickd.com and the Joystickd iOS app

What Joystickd stores

Your email address and password (handled by our database provider, Supabase — we never see your password), your username, optional profile picture and bio, and everything you create here: game logs, ratings, reviews, comments, likes, follows, lists, award ballots and wishlist entries.

If you turn on notifications in the iOS app, we also store your device's push token so Apple can deliver notifications (likes, comments and new followers) to your phone. Turning notifications off, signing out, or deleting the app removes it from use.

What we don't do

No ads. No selling or sharing your data. No tracking beyond what's needed to run the site. Emails are only used for sign-in, confirmation and password resets.

Who can see what

Your profile, logs, ratings, reviews, lists and comments are public. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase — database, accounts and sign-in
  • IGDB — game titles, artwork and details
  • OpenCritic — critic scores shown beside community scores
  • Brevo — sends confirmation and password-reset emails
  • GitHub Pages & Cloudflare — hosting

These providers process data only to provide the functions above.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete my account permanently removes your account and everything you've posted — logs, reviews, comments, follows, ballots, wishlist and your avatar. This can't be undone. You can also email us (below) and we'll delete it for you.

Age

Joystickd is intended for users aged 13 and up.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Questions or requests: joystickdapp@gmail.com
