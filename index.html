Community guidelines

Joystickd is for talking about games with people who love them. Keep it that way:

  • Be decent. No harassment, threats, slurs, or hate toward anyone, in reviews or comments.
  • No spam. No ads, referral links, or posting the same thing across many games.
  • Rate honestly. Review-bombing or brigading a game isn't permitted.
  • Your account is yours. One person, one account; no impersonating others.

Breaking these can get content removed or an account closed. Use the Report option on any review or comment to flag a problem.

Privacy

What Joystickd stores: your email address and password (handled by our database provider, Supabase — we never see your password), your username, optional profile picture and bio, and everything you create here: logs, ratings, reviews, comments, likes, follows, award ballots and wishlist entries.

Notifications: if you turn them on in the iOS app, we also store your device's push token so Apple can deliver notifications to your phone. Signing out or deleting the app stops them.

Data sources: game details and artwork come from IGDB; critic scores from OpenCritic.

What we don't do: no ads, no selling or sharing your data, no tracking beyond what's needed to run the site. Emails are only used for sign-in, confirmation and password resets.

Who can see what: your profile, logs, reviews and comments are public. Your email is never shown to anyone.

Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) permanently removes your account and everything you've posted. This can't be undone.

Delete your account
