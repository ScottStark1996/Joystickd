Community guidelines

Joystickd is for talking about games with people who love them. Keep it that way:

  • Be decent. No harassment, threats, slurs, or hate toward anyone, in reviews or comments.
  • No spam. No ads, referral links, or posting the same thing across many games.
  • Rate honestly. Review-bombing or brigading a game isn't permitted.
  • Your account is yours. One person, one account; no impersonating others.

Breaking these can get content removed or an account closed. Use the Report option on any review or comment to flag a problem.
