Common questions

I didn't get my confirmation or reset email. Check your junk/spam folder — they sometimes land there. The sender is noreply@joystickd.com.

How do I reset my password? Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, or change it any time in Settings → Change password.

How do I report a review or comment? Tap the ⚑ Report button next to it. Reports go straight to moderation.

How do I block someone? Open their profile and tap Block. Manage your blocks in Settings → Blocked users.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete my account. It permanently removes your account and everything you've posted.
